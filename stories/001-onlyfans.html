1
Slot one — stated so you can attack it
The claim

The claim under test is an only-way claim: that OnlyFans, or something exactly like it, had to happen. Three systems are said to have converged and made it so. A movement that installed the vocabulary of bodily sovereignty. A pandemic that concentrated savings and displaced women from service work. Payment infrastructure that had quietly matured. Remove any one, the story says, and the explosion never comes.

That story is unfalsifiable as written, and this trade is chronically suspected of telling exactly that kind of story. So the claim is restated as something attackable: for each proposed cause, is it structure — remove it and the thing stops — or scaffolding, loud while the thing went up, gone when you look back? The instrument that decides is the DeStrange, and its standing question is: how many branches did you run?

2
Slot two — before any result
Sample & window
Fifteen creator-monetization platforms, 2013–2026. Breakout threshold: ≥1M creators or ≥$1B annual gross volume within six years of launch. Coding rules frozen before outcomes were looked at.

The comparison set: OnlyFans, Patreon, Substack, Twitch, Cameo, Fansly, JustForFans, ManyVids, Chaturbate, MyFreeCams, Pornhub's model program, Tumblr, Gumroad, Ko-fi, and AVN Stars — which folded in 2022 and is the most valuable row in the table, because a necessity test with no failures in it is worthless. Case list and coding rules are committed with a timestamp before any outcome is coded; that one paragraph of pre-registration is the cheapest possible cure for a story assembled around its ending.

4
Slot four — what the test did to the prior
The finding, with the surprise

The opening hypothesis does not survive its own test. Three co-equal systems goes in; one structure and two scaffolds come out. Remove the vocabulary and the cam economy is already there, profitable, six years early — MeToo downgrades from cause to legitimacy accelerant for the mainstream wave. Remove the pandemic and the curve keeps its slope and loses its spike. Remove the rails and the thing stops: minus 78 percent in a day next door, eight days from policy to surrender at home.

Scaffolding fools retrodiction because it is loud while the building goes up, and gone by the time you look back.

And the pioneer claim inverts. OnlyFans did not invent the creator economy; Patreon had proved the mechanism in 2013. What OnlyFans did was apply a solved mechanism to the one category every incumbent's terms of service had fenced off. The inevitability, such as it was, belonged to the gap — a proven model, a excluded population, and the only question being who would be willing.

Slot five — in public, not under questioning
The caveat

The load-bearing verdict rides on one eight-day crisis that was reversed: a near-death, observed once, is inference, not proof. The creator counts are aggregator-sourced until the Companies House triangulation lands. The trend-break projection is illustrative until the pre-2020 series is harvested. And a condition table with ? cells has, by definition, not yet been run — the falsification sentence above is live, and it may fire.

The classic register · signal: OnlyFans · consumption/behavioral · grounded
The excavation
One line: when a movement handed women the vocabulary, a lockdown handed them the audience, and the rails were already laid, the platform's only remaining job was to be willing.
Move 1 — decode the sign
Ownership, at last

The sign says: your body, your terms, your business — the studio, the agency, and the algorithm all fired at once. The myth sold to participants and audience alike is disintermediation as empowerment.

Move 2 — name the code
The processor's veto

The rule set producing the sign is not feminism or lockdown boredom; it is which work a bank will process. Every incumbent's ToS fenced off the category; the code stays invisible because processors prefer it that way.

Move 3 — expose the gap
Rented independence

Sign promise: ownership without gatekeepers. System delivery: access contingent on banking tolerance, decided in eight days in Aug 2021, at 20% rent. Gap reframe: OnlyFans is not replacing the gatekeepers. It is replacing the studio's cut with the processor's veto.

The adoption timeline 2016 → 2027 Emergence → inflection → capital infrastructure → mainstream crossing → current status. The same timeline the instrument reads for shocks.
Diffusion path

Cam-adjacent professionals (the pre-2016 base) → service and gig workers displaced by the she-cession → influencers with importable Instagram/TikTok audiences (the Spring 2020 social-proof wave) → mainstream celebrity stunts, 2021.

Socioeconomic anchor

Primary: lockdowns concentrated spendable money (savings rate 33%, April 2020, a BEA record) while displacing the female-majority service workforce (women were 55% of April's 20.5M job losses). Secondary: lockdown privatized consumption — the social-surveillance cost of subscribing collapsed. Tertiary: the rails were already frictionless.

Virality mechanism

Complex contagion on the creator side — joining required multiple reinforcing exposures and social proof (Centola 2010) — with winner-take-all outcome distribution on the earnings side (Salganik, Dodds & Watts 2006). Identity split: informational adoption (income) and identity-performative adoption (the destigmatized-entrepreneur persona; Van Bavel & Packer).

Five avenues, compact

Network: hub amplification via celebrity joins, winner-take-all topology. Contagion: complex, bridge-width dependent — each join widened the bridge for second-degree ties. Statistical physics: homophily bred creator subcultures that later seeded the counter-signal. Micro-motives: a Schelling cascade — mild individual decisions, non-linear collective outcome, tipping ~Spring 2020. Generative test: the micro-rules (direct pay, subscription, 20% cut, no feed) grow the macro outcome; the near-ban shows the rules' emergent behavior surprising the platform itself.

Counter-signals

Within-frame: creator disavowal and migration toward owned infrastructure — private Discords, direct subscriptions — after the 2021 rent-and-rails scare. Exit: the platform-labor reframe: union talk, gig-work analogies, rights language reconstructing the employment protections the empowerment frame skipped. Exit is the stronger bet, and it is the one the forward run measures.

The empowerment was real, and rented. The landlord was a payment processor.

Strategist: position against rails-risk, not on empowerment copy — the gap is where the durable story is. Creator: the exit bet beats the within-frame bet; own the rail relationship or own nothing. Researcher: the leading indicator is processor policy language and the L:E framing ratio — both move before the metrics do.

Glossary

QCA — Qualitative Comparative Analysis; codes cases as condition sets to test necessity/sufficiency in small samples. Consistency — how reliably a condition accompanies the outcome (≥0.90 ≈ necessary). Coverage — how much of the outcome the condition explains; guards against trivial necessity. Natural experiment — reality removes a variable so you don't have to. Interrupted time series — fit the pre-shock trend, project, subtract. Complex contagion — adoption requiring multiple reinforcing exposures. Schelling cascade — mild local preferences, extreme collective outcome. Structure / scaffolding — remove it and the thing stops, versus remove it and the thing merely slows. Pre-registration — freezing rules before outcomes are looked at. Sign / code / gap — what it signals, the rule set producing it, and the distance between promise and delivery.
